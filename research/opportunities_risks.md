# Opportunities and Risks – ClearPath

People often worry that AI can be misleading. ClearPath is designed to prevent this. For every risk, here is exactly how ClearPath handles it:

| Opportunity | Risk | Solution – how ClearPath prevents it |
|---|---|---|
| Answer everyday questions instantly, 24/7 | AI could give wrong or outdated information | **1.** ClearPath only uses official sources (government, cities, embassies) – not the AI's own memory. **2.** A fact-check step compares every sentence with the source before the user sees it. **3.** Every answer shows the source link, so the user can check it. **4.** If no official answer is found, ClearPath says so and names the right office – it never guesses. **5.** Every week, ClearPath checks if official pages have changed and updates its knowledge. |
| Explain official letters and forms in simple words | Letters contain personal data (names, addresses, sometimes health or money details) | **1.** No account or login needed. **2.** Letter photos are processed and deleted immediately – never stored. **3.** Only the information needed for the answer is used (GDPR data minimisation). |
| Help in 10 languages, by voice and text | A translation could change the meaning | **1.** The answer is created and fact-checked **once**, then translated – so the facts are the same in every language. **2.** Important official terms stay in German too, e.g. "address registration (Anmeldung)", so the user can show them at the office. **3.** Deadlines and numbers are copied exactly, not rewritten. |
| Consultants spend more time on complex cases | Users could trust the AI too much in serious situations | **1.** Emergencies: ClearPath always shows "Call 112 / 110" first. **2.** Legal, medical or complex cases are clearly handed over to a human expert. **3.** Every answer says what ClearPath does *not* decide. |
| Show nearby services (hospitals, pharmacies, offices) | Location is personal data | **1.** Location is used only with the user's permission. **2.** An approximate location is enough. **3.** The location is never stored. **4.** Users can type an address instead. |
| Fair, consistent help for everyone | Answers could be unfair or biased towards some groups | **1.** The same official sources are used for everyone – the facts don't change depending on who asks. **2.** One answer is created, then translated – so content is equal in all languages. **3.** Testing: the same questions are asked in different languages, and the answers are compared. |

## What we avoid on purpose
- **No decisions about people:** ClearPath never decides on visas, benefits, jobs or legal cases. That would be high-risk under the EU AI Act.
- **No medical or legal advice:** only general information and the right contact.
- **No automatic actions:** ClearPath never sends forms or messages on its own; the user decides.
