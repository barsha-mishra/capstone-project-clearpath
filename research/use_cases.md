# Use Cases – ClearPath

Three use cases for Chleo's medium-sized everyday-support company (about 80 employees).

## Use case 1: Ask anything – step-by-step guidance (main use case)
- **Who:** Anyone living in Germany with an everyday problem (e.g. lost passport, lost job, address registration, insurance, car, school).
- **Problem:** People don't know where to go or what to do. In an official survey, citizens rated "understanding the law" and "online services" as the **two worst parts** of dealing with authorities (Destatis, Lebenslagenbefragung 2023).
- **What the AI does:** The user speaks or types the problem in one of 10 languages. ClearPath answers with a clear step-by-step plan – which office, which documents, deadlines – based only on official sources, with source links, by voice and text.
- **Value for Chleo:** Routine questions are answered instantly, 24/7. Consultants have more time for complex cases.
- **Why it fits a medium company:** Uses existing AI services (no own AI model needed); one IT person can run it.

## Use case 2: Post scanner – understand official letters
- **Who:** Anyone who receives a letter they don't understand (tax office, health insurance, job agency, fines).
- **Problem:** Many people find official letters hard to understand – citizens rated them **below average** in the same official survey (Destatis, Lebenslagenbefragung 2023).
- **What the AI does:** The user takes a photo of the letter. ClearPath explains what it is, labels it as "Action needed", "Deadline" or "Just keep", and lists the next steps.
- **Value for Chleo:** A very common, repeated request answered in seconds instead of a consultant appointment.
- **Why it fits a medium company:** Same core system as use case 1, just with photo input – low extra cost.

## Use case 3: Arrival guide and "Near me"
- **Who:** People new to Germany, or new to a city.
- **Problem:** About 14.07 million foreign citizens live in Germany (Destatis, end of 2025); newcomers don't know the first steps or where essential services are.
- **What the AI does:** A checklist for the first hours, weeks and month (e.g. address registration, health insurance, bank account), plus nearby hospitals, pharmacies, supermarkets, schools, Kitas and offices using open map data (OpenStreetMap).
- **Value for Chleo:** Attracts new clients early – the moment they need help most.
- **Why it fits a medium company:** Uses free open data; no expensive data licences.

## Recommendation
**Start with use case 1** (Ask anything). It solves the biggest problem, is the core of the app, and use cases 2 and 3 are built on top of it. This is the focus of the POC (proof of concept) and MVP.
